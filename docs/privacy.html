Privacy Policy for Vivian Nguyen Projects LinkedIn Blogger

Last updated: 2026-07-27

This privacy policy covers the personal LinkedIn automation app ("the app") used to draft and publish posts to a single LinkedIn account: the account of the app's owner.

Who this app is for

The app is a personal tool. It is operated by and for one person, the account owner. It is not a product, service, or platform offered to other people, and it has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app through LinkedIn, the app receives:

  • An OAuth access token that lets it post on the owner's behalf (the w_member_social permission).
  • The owner's LinkedIn member ID (via openid and profile), used only to address posts to the owner's own feed.

The app does not access the owner's connections, messages, contact lists, or any other members' data.

How data is used and stored

  • The access token and member ID are stored locally on the owner's own computer, in files that are never shared or transmitted anywhere except to LinkedIn's official API when publishing a post.
  • Post text is generated from notes the owner writes, and is sent only to LinkedIn (to publish) and to the owner's chosen AI drafting provider (to draft the wording).
  • No data is collected from, or shared with, any third party for advertising, analytics, or resale.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are held only until they expire or the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from LinkedIn account settings, and can delete the locally stored token files at any time.
